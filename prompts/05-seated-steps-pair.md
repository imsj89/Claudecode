## What the two prompts share, and why

Everything above `POSE` is identical in both: `SUBJECT`, `FACE`, `WARDROBE`, `BODY`, and
everything below is the same `SCENE` and `SKIN`. Only `POSE`, `CAMERA` and `LIGHT` differ.

That is deliberate. Identity, wardrobe and body have each drifted in this project when left to
the model, and the fix every time was carrying the block rather than trusting a reference:

- **FACE** is the canonical lock from CHARACTER-BIBLE §2, condensed. Positive statements of
  what each feature is — a lock written as a do-not list is a hit list (§5b).
- **WARDROBE** is the locked summer look plus the black skirt. `exact` is load-bearing:
  clothing described inline as scene flavour drifts between seeds.
- **BODY** carries even with a reference attached. The reference biases the silhouette; it does
  not hold it. Every shot that drifted was one where this block was missing.
- **SKIN** is the texture pass built into the generation rather than run afterward, so there is
  no second edit round to survive. It contains pores, surface tooth, specular break-up and
  subsurface only — the four items on the safe side of the scale rule.

**No vellus hair in either prompt.** Enlarged ten times it is visible facial hair, and it has no
safe enlarged form on her face (§5b). `Her face is smooth-shaven and hairless` carries instead.

## How the candid is built

A candid is not a pose described as spontaneous. It is **a photograph that failed slightly**,
and prompt 2 specifies the failures rather than the feeling:

- **The photographer is late.** `reacted a fraction too late` and `the focus stayed where she
  used to be` — her face softer than the step behind her. This one detail does more than any
  amount of "candid, natural, unposed", because it is a thing only a real camera does.
- **The moment is mid-action, not its peak.** Her hand is `reaching for the step and not yet
  touching it`; the drink is surging but has not spilled. Models default to the resolved
  instant; naming the unresolved one is what buys the motion.
- **The frame is badly held.** Tilted horizon, uneven headroom, one knee cut by the edge. A
  well-composed photograph of a fall still reads as a photoshoot.
- **The blur is selective.** Hand, ponytail and drink surface move; the step does not.

Prompt 1 uses the same logic more quietly: she leans down to the straw instead of raising the
cup, which is what people actually do with a full drink and a lid.

## What to check on the output

1. **Identity** — same woman as the reference. Brows, the mole above her left eyebrow, the
   grey iris with its darker ring.
2. **Body proportions** — narrow waist and shoulders against full hips and bust. This is the
   block most often silently averaged away.
3. **Wardrobe complete** — crop tank, black skirt, huggie hoops, coral-and-turquoise pendant
   *and* ring, sunglasses on her head, high ponytail.
4. **Hands.** Seated poses put both hands in frame and prompt 2 splays the fingers of one.
   Count them.
5. **Background faces stay indistinct.** Any resolved stranger's face means re-roll.
6. **Skin** — pores present, sheen broken into small glints rather than one gloss, tone even,
   face hairless, no lines beyond what the pose folds.
7. **Age** — reads mid-twenties (PROJECT-BRIEF §7). An open-mouthed laugh from slightly above
   is a babyface-prone combination.
8. **Prompt 2 only: is the fall legible?** If she reads as sitting normally, strengthen the
   pose block before touching anything else — tipped past recovery, hips sliding, hand not yet
   landed.

### What was cut, and what could not be

Seedream carries less prompt weight than Nano Banana Pro — past forty comma-separated clauses
nothing lands (BIBLE §5b) — so these run at roughly half length.

**Cut:** the full face description, most capture artifacts, the scene inventory, the subsurface
and surface-tooth blocks, and all prose connective tissue.

**Kept, and not negotiable:**

| Kept | Why |
|---|---|
| `A single full-bleed vertical photograph filling the entire frame edge to edge` | The only thing that suppresses rendered bezels and inset thumbnails. Negatives do not work |
| The whole BODY line | The Element biases the silhouette, it does not hold it. Every drifted shot was one missing this |
| Brows, grey iris with darker ring, the mole | The three identity details that have actually drifted. The Element carries the rest |
| `smooth-shaven and hairless` | Vellus hair enlarged is facial hair |
| `mature adult proportions` | PROJECT-BRIEF §7 hard guardrail |
| `Deep depth of field … no portrait mode` | The loudest AI tell in a phone photo |
| The strangers line | An empty summer sidewalk reads staged, and resolved background faces are the worst failure |
| Pores plus broken sheen | The two skin items that survive at this length |

**Which model to use.** Nano Banana Pro has held identity, framing and wardrobe through every
pass in this project; Seedream re-rendered a frame it was asked to edit. The reason to run
Seedream is Unlimited — free re-rolls on a pose that will need several. A reasonable split is
to explore the pose on Seedream until the composition is right, then run the keeper on Nano
Banana Pro with the long prompt above.
