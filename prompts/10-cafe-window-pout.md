### Corrections made across three review passes

1. **Gaze.** The first draft said the irises were "pushed toward the outer corners."
   That is only half true — when both eyes look the same way, one iris moves to its outer
   corner and the other to its inner. Rewritten directionally: *"looking off to her left
   … both irises shifted to the same side so that white shows on the opposite side of
   each eye."*
2. **Head tilt, verified twice.** Checked against the eye line rather than the hair mass:
   her left eye sits lower in the frame, so the crown leans toward her left shoulder.
3. **Negations dropped.** "not sad, and not smiling" nominated a smile. Replaced with the
   positive *"Her mouth stays closed in the pout."*
4. **Table position contradicted itself** — "to her right" in LOCATION against "lower
   right" in CAMERA. With the camera facing her, her right is frame-left. Softened to
   "beside her".
5. **Spelled-out signage backfires.** A draft read *"spelled exactly m-a-c-y-s"*; a
   typography-strong model will draw the hyphens. Changed to *"five letters, white,
   lowercase, sans-serif … correctly spelled."*
6. **Removed the blue parking sign** from the street description, because it contradicted
   the closing line that the storefront sign is the only lettering in the picture.

  same angle and height but widened to roughly lap-up, because the cappuccino, the pastry
  and the "macys" sign all had to be in frame.
