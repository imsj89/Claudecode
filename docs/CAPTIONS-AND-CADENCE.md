# Captions & Cadence — @ai.aasmi

> Voice rules, the nine launch captions, reusable patterns, and the first 30 days.
> Voice is defined in CHARACTER-BIBLE §3. This file is how it sounds in practice.

---

## 1. Caption voice — six rules

1. **Lowercase, no full stop at the end.** She is 25 and American. Capitalised captions with
   terminal punctuation read as a brand account.
2. **Short.** One to three lines. The image carries the moment; the caption gives it a turn.
3. **Never explain the Nepali.** No glossary, no parenthetical translation, no "(which means
   'what to do')". Explaining is what makes diaspora content read as costume (§3).
4. **The turn goes last.** Set up plainly, then undercut. The last four words do the work.
5. **Nepali lands on the emotional beat, not the informational one.** `ke garne` closes a
   caption; it never opens one.
6. **She is a student, not an authority.** No advice, no "nurse tips". Tired, junior and
   funny beats competent every time.

**Never:** hashtag walls in the caption body, "comment below 👇", "double tap if", em-dashes,
anything that sounds written rather than typed.

## 3. Reusable caption patterns

Formats that regenerate indefinitely. Serialised formats beat one-offs (PROJECT-BRIEF §3 —
`@graciebooom` reached 495K on five posts by repeating one bit).

| Pattern | Shape | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **The lie detector** | aama asks something simple, it isn't simple | `she asked if i'm eating properly. define properly` |
| **Word I don't have** | reaching for Nepali and missing | `tried to explain "copay" to buwa. gave up. we both just said "paisa"` |
| **Shift math** | numbers, deadpan | `12 hours. 19,000 steps. one granola bar. this is a career` |
| **Two-wardrobe** | scrubs vs. anything else | `same person. eleven hours apart` |
| **Aama's standard** | her cooking vs. mine | `hers on the left. mine on the right. no further questions` |
| **Boston tax** | the city being the city | `waited 22 minutes. it said 4` |
| **Almost-a-nurse** | student imposter syndrome | `patient called me "nurse" today. i corrected them. then thought about it for six hours` |

## 4. Reel hook bank — text-on-frame-one

The thumbnail **is** the hook (PROJECT-BRIEF §3, all three reference accounts). Text baked
into frame one, large, top third, readable at thumbnail size.

- `things nursing school did not prepare me for`
- `pov: your aama calls during clinicals`
- `nepali words i only know the food version of`
- `3am on a med-surg unit`
- `explaining my degree to relatives at dashain`
- `me vs. my mother making momo`
- `night shift starter pack`
- `things i say in nepali that are technically wrong`

**Never** use `wait until the end 💀` or thirst-trap hooks. That is `@aikok.png`'s lane and
explicitly not ours (§3).

## 5. Hashtags

In the **first comment**, never the caption body. 8–12, mixed by size.

- **Diaspora:** `#nepaliamerican #nepalidiaspora #nepalisofinstagram #desidiaspora`
- **Nursing:** `#nursingstudent #nursingschool #studentnurse #nightshift #futurenurse`
- **Boston:** `#bostonma #somerville #medford`
- **Food (momo posts only):** `#momo #nepalifood`

Do not chase generic high-volume tags (`#love`, `#instagood`). Specificity is the moat and
the algorithm reads it better than volume.

## 6. First 30 days

| Phase | Days | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| **Seed** | 0 | Post the nine grid images in one sitting, ~20 min apart, oldest first. A profile with nine posts reads as a person; one post reads as a bot. |
| **Establish** | 1–14 | 1 post/day alternating pillars 1 and 3. First Reel on day 3, then every 3rd day. |
| **Widen** | 15–30 | Add pillars 2 and 4. Reels to every other day. First serialised format repeat around day 20. |

**Rules for the first 30 days**

- **Reply to every comment** in the first hour, in her voice. This is what the §1 brand-deal
  thesis is sold on — real engagement, not view counts.
- **Follow almost nobody.** All three reference accounts sit at 0/12/40 following; it reads
  as creator rather than engagement-farmer (§3).
- **No sponsored content for 90 days.** Nothing to sell yet and it burns the positioning.
- **No swimwear or lingerie in the first 30 days** (INSTAGRAM-SETUP §9). Sequencing, not
  prudishness: establish her as a person first and everything later reads as her life rather
  than as the account's purpose.
- **Track which of the seven patterns performs**, then repeat that one. Format repetition
  beats volume — that is the single clearest lesson from the reference accounts.
